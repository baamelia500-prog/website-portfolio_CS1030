Digital Art Programs

There are many different programs that artists can use to make digital art. Some are free, while others cost money.

Popular Art Programs

MediBang Paint

MediBang Paint is a free art program that can be used for drawing, painting, and comics.

MediBang Website

Clip Studio Paint

Clip Studio Paint is a paid art program used for drawing, comics, illustrations, and animation.

Clip Studio Website

Things to Look For

  • Price
  • Brushes
  • Drawing tools
  • Animation tools
  • Ease of use
Tip: Free programs can be a great place to start if you are new to digital art.
